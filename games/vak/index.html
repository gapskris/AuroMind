Privacy Policy

1. About Vak

Welcome to Vak: The Control of Speech (presented broadly as Vak — The Art of Conscious Speech), an educational and contemplative word puzzle application belonging to the AuroMind series. Vak is designed to help players cultivate linguistic precision, conscious speech restraint, noble virtues, and inner quietude through contemplative gameplay, anagram wheels, and reflective journal themes.

2. Information We Store on Your Device

Vak is an offline-first application. We do not operate user accounts, cloud sign-in services, or remote player databases. All gameplay progress, vocabulary discoveries, and personal reflections are stored exclusively in your device's local storage (localStorage and IndexedDB).

  • Player Profile & Seeker Name: Stored in vak_seeker_name and vak_player_profile_v1 to personalize reflections.
  • Game Progression: Completed levels across Wisdom (1–407), Mandalas (1–361), and Cosmos (1–600).
  • Sparks Balance: In-game points earned purely through mindful gameplay (vak_sparks_balance). Sparks cannot be purchased with real money.
  • Evening Reflections: Self-guided ratings for Truthfulness (Satya), Goodwill (Ahimsa), and Quietude (Mouna) in auro_vak_reflections.
  • Preferences: Theme choice, Senior View (+25% font scale), reduced motion, and audio volume toggles.
  • Local Diagnostic Log: Capped at the last 100 events in auro_vak_telemetry_events purely for local export; never automatically uploaded.

3. Information Transmitted Through External Services

Because Vak requires no account, we do not collect your name, email, physical location, contacts, or financial credentials. However, when your device is connected to the internet, technical connection requests occur as described below for typography fonts.

4. Google Fonts and Third-Party Services

Vak utilizes typography from Google Fonts (Cinzel, Outfit, Playfair Display) to render aesthetic lettering.

  • Connection Destination: When online, font stylesheets and binaries are requested over HTTPS from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com.
  • Technical Data: Your device communicates standard network connection metadata to Google LLC (IP address, browser User-Agent, HTTP headers).
  • Scope: Vak sends no player names, progress, or reflections to Google. If offline, the app falls back to system fonts without error.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

  • 0% Advertisements: Zero banner ads, zero interstitial popups, zero video ads, and zero rewarded ad networks. No ad SDKs are included.
  • 0% Third-Party Analytics: No Google Analytics for Firebase, Mixpanel, Segment, or Facebook SDK.
  • 0% Tracking Identifiers: The app does not request or use the Android Advertising ID (AAID) or cross-app tracking IDs.

6. How Your Information Is Used

Local data is used solely to maintain game state, compute unlocked letters/words, render Sparks, and preserve your accessibility preferences. We do not use your information for profiling, behavioral targeting, or automated decision-making.

7. Data Retention

Developer retention is zero days (no records are held on developer servers). On-device records persist until you erase them or uninstall the app.

8. How to Delete Local Information

You can erase all local data at any time:

  • In-App Purge: Open Settings (⚙️) > Tap "⚠️ Reset & Purge Data" > Type PURGE to permanently drop local storage and AuroVakDB.
  • Android Settings: Go to Settings > Apps > Vak: The Control of Speech > Storage > Clear Storage.

Note: Deleting local data removes all records from your device, but cannot retroactively delete historical technical network logs (such as IP addresses) that may have been recorded by external CDN servers (Google Fonts) during network font requests.

9. Children's Privacy

Vak is suitable for all ages, including families and children. We do not knowingly solicit personal data from children under 13. The app includes no multi-user chat, social sharing, camera access, microphone access, or location tracking.

10. Security

Player data is protected by Android application sandboxing. Vak includes an optional backup feature protected by PBKDF2 password derivation and AES-256-GCM encryption. External network font requests use encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3).

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Updates will be reflected within this in-app policy and on our official public hosting page with an updated Effective Date.
