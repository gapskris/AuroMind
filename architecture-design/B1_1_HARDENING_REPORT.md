## 1. Hardening Summary

All mandatory defects (`A-1`, `A-2`, `B-1`, `B-2`, `C-1`, `C-2`, `C-3`, `D-1`, and `E-1`) from the Independent B1 Review have been resolved while preserving the v1.0.0 architecture, local-first offline-first design, and zero-dependency browser-native ES module structure. Low-severity dead code (`A-3`) in `idbPut()` was also removed cleanly.

During B1.1 test closure, the newly added realistic mid-flight storage mutation test (Test #17 Case 17b) also exposed a real write-ordering hazard inside `persistWinnerSafely()` where an async `await idbPut(...)` followed by an unguarded synchronous `lsSetRaw(...)` could overwrite an earlier record written to `localStorage` mid-flight. A synchronous pre-`localStorage` re-check plus bounded post-write convergence verification was added to close that race completely.

## 3. Non-Mandatory Low-Severity Items Observed (Not Modified in B1.1)

The following Low-severity notes from the B1 Independent Review were outside the mandatory B1.1 scope and were intentionally left untouched to preserve strict scope discipline:

- **A-4 (Low):** `validateGuestRecord()` does not enforce an arbitrary minimum-year plausibility floor on `createdAt`.
- **C-4 (Low):** `registerProviders()` remains callable post-`init()` (inert while all Tier-2 capabilities default to `false`; should be sealed before Tier-2 providers are enabled).
- **F-1 (Low):** `.github/workflows/pages.yml` was not modified in B1.1; its `_site/assets/` copy already stages `/assets/sdk/*.js`.
